Terms of Use

SsPdfEditor is a free PDF reading and editing tool that runs locally in your browser.

The application is completely free for all users. It has no hidden fees, subscriptions, usage quotas or locked features. User Data Responsibility

Because your documents are processed on your own device, backing up and storing your files is under your control. We recommend keeping a backup before irreversible operations (redaction, page deletion).

Limitation of Liability

The software is provided as is, with no warranty of fitness for a particular purpose or of uninterrupted operation. The developers cannot be held liable for any problems that may arise from its use.

Limits of the Tools

A drawn, typed or photographed signature is not a certified digital signature. The PDF/A and PDF/UA checks are automated and do not stand in for a conformance certificate. Conversions keep the content of a document, not its exact look. Deleting a draft is not secure erasure. The report of each operation says what was approximated and what could not be done; check the result yourself on important documents.
