Free and open source · No account · Works offline too

Edit text in place.

Click a paragraph and rewrite it; the text reflows within its own box and the rest of the page stays where it is. The old glyphs are really removed and the result is checked by a second PDF engine. Only horizontal text can be edited; blocks that cannot are marked.

Mark up. Take notes. Draw.

Highlight, underline, strikeout, sticky notes, freehand pen, shapes and stamps. Typed text embeds Noto Sans, so every reader shows its letters right. Distance, perimeter and area measurements are written as real PDF annotations.

Not a black box. Real removal.

Permanent redaction deletes the text operators and glyphs in the marked area from the PDF stream, along with the form fields, comments and links under it. The output is checked again glyph by glyph, and the report lists remaining terms and earlier revisions.

Drag your pages.

Select, drag, rotate, duplicate or delete pages in the organize grid; every step can be undone. Extracting, splitting by range, size or bookmarks and merging live here too.

Fill in. Sign.

Fill in form fields, draw, type or photograph your signature and place it on the page. That is a picture; to prove the document has not changed, sign it with your PKCS#12 identity as a PAdES B-B digital signature.

A scanned page, searchable text.

OCR adds an invisible, selectable and searchable text layer to scanned pages. 27 languages are supported; the engine and language packs are served from this site and work offline after their first use.

Word, Excel, PowerPoint: PDF.

Drop DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, HTML, text, CSV, EPUB or FB2 files on the editor; they are converted to PDF in the browser and open in a new tab. Conversion keeps the content, not the exact page layout. PDF to Word, Excel and CSV is there too.

Your documents live only on your device.

0 bytes over the network

Your files are never transferred, copied or stored on any remote server.

No account or sign-up

You do not need to provide an email address, create an account or sign in.

Save straight to disk

In Chromium-based browsers you can write your documents directly back to the original file on your computer; in every browser you can download a copy.

Drafts stay in this browser

Unsaved work is kept as a draft in this browser so it is not lost; documents opened with a password and sensitive sessions leave no draft. The Privacy page lists what is kept.

No third-party requests

The engines and fonts are served from this site, and a strict content security policy (CSP) allows connections to this site only. There are no cookies and no analytics.

Open source

The source code is open on GitHub under AGPL-3.0-or-later, so you can check what it does.

And there is more.

Reading and review

Continuous, single-page, book and full-screen presentation; search that highlights matches, bookmarks, a magnifier and reading mode. Read aloud uses only the voices installed on your device.

Comments and review

Threaded replies and review states are real PDF records, so Acrobat and Foxit show the same thread. Import and export as XFDF, FDF and JSON.

Forms and XFA

Fill in, create and flatten AcroForm fields; detect the fields of a flat form. Static XFA is filled in, dynamic XFA opens in its own window; XFA scripts do not run.

Digital signing and verification

PAdES B-B signatures with your PKCS#12 identity. Verification reports integrity, trust, revocation and coverage separately; revocation data is never fetched from the network.

Scan with the camera

Make a PDF with a phone or laptop camera or from photos: the corners are found, the page is straightened, and the image never leaves your device.

Encryption and passwords

AES-256 passwords and permissions; the output is reopened and verified. A document opened with a password leaves no draft on the device.

Accessibility and PDF/UA

A 34-rule check modeled on the Matterhorn Protocol, a tag and reading-order editor and alternative text. No score and no conformance claim.

PDF/A, compression, repair

Conversion to and checks for PDF/A-2b, 3b and 1b; a file that breaks a rule is never delivered. Lossless or lossy compression and repair of broken PDFs.

Sanitize

Removes scripts, attachments, metadata, private application data, thumbnails and hidden layers; the result is read back and verified.

Structure and stamping

Properties and XMP, bookmarks, attachments, layers; header, footer, page numbers, Bates numbering and watermarks.

Compare and batch

Compare two documents as text or pixels; run a sequence of steps on up to 256 files at once; N-up, booklet and poster printing.

Workflow

A home screen ordered by task, the Ctrl+K command palette, undo history and local drafts; save over the original file in Chromium-based browsers.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about SsPdfEditor.

Are my files really not uploaded to any server?
Yes. You can check by opening your browser's Network tab. When you open or save a PDF, not a single byte of data is sent out. Everything happens entirely on your own computer's hardware.
How can I work without an internet connection?
The first time you open the editor, its start page, interface text and fonts are saved to your browser cache. Settings → Offline use → Prepare downloads the rest ahead: every tool's code, the PDF engines and the fonts. Once it reports ready, you can open, edit and export a PDF in airplane mode, even on a device that has never opened a PDF online. OCR language packs and the PDF/A converter work offline after one use online. Without Prepare, a tool you have not used on this device needs the internet the first time.
Why is this service 100% free?
Because all document processing runs on the user's own device, there is no expensive server-side cloud compute cost. That is why no subscription fee or restriction is needed.
How do I save the file I edited?
In desktop browsers such as Chrome or Edge you can save directly over your original file (Save). In Firefox or Safari you can download the edited result as a new file with one click (Export).
Which file and page sizes are supported?
All standard PDF documents are supported. On a computer, documents of up to 2,000 pages and 300 MB open; above 1,500 pages the editor warns you. Phones and tablets have the same ceiling, but documents over 300 pages or 64 MB open for viewing only. A batch run takes at most 256 files and 1 GiB in total at a time.
Which languages does it support?
The interface is in Turkish and English; change it in Settings → Language. OCR works in 27 languages (the list is in the Features section). The OCR engine and every language pack are served by this site and downloaded the first time you use them; after that they work offline too.
Can I open Word, Excel or PowerPoint files?
Yes. DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, HTML, text/Markdown, CSV/TSV, EPUB and FB2 files are converted to PDF in the browser when you open them or drop them on the editor, and the result opens in a new tab as real, selectable and searchable text. Conversion keeps the content of the document, not its exact page layout, fonts or formatting; each report says what was approximated. The old DOC, XLS and PPT formats, OpenDocument and RTF are not converted. You can also export a PDF to Word, Excel or CSV; a PDF records no paragraphs or tables, so these are rebuilt from the page and can be wrong. In the exact Word layout a scanned page is read with OCR (Turkish and English unless you choose others), so its text becomes editable; words OCR was unsure of get a Word comment.
Is redaction really permanent?
Yes. Marked text is not covered with a black box; it is removed from the PDF content, together with any form field, comment or link under the marked area, the file is rewritten and the output is re-checked glyph by glyph. The audit report lists any remaining terms, earlier revisions and leftover structure. Sanitize removes what the pages do not show (scripts, attachments, metadata, hidden layers) and verifies the result by reading it back; a digital signature does not survive it. A limit: the audit scans raw bytes and the file's text strings (hex and UTF-16 included), so it cannot see inside compressed streams, and the report says so.
What is the difference between a simple signature and a digital signature?
A drawn, typed or photographed signature is a picture placed on the page; it proves nothing about who signed or whether the document changed afterwards, and the dialog says so. For that, sign with your PKCS#12 identity to get a PAdES B-B signature. A new signature carries no timestamp and no revocation data, because there is no network to ask. When verifying, trust rests only on certificates you imported and revocation is read only from lists on your device and from revocation data embedded in the PDF itself; with neither, the answer is “unknown”.
Can I scan a document with my phone?
Yes: File → Scan with camera (also on the home screen and in the Ctrl+K palette). On a phone it opens the rear camera, the picture never leaves your device and the camera runs only while you scan. With no camera, or if you refuse permission, you can choose photo files instead. The camera needs a secure connection (https or localhost). The page is found from its edges and straightened; the output is not meant for archival reproduction. You can run OCR on the resulting PDF to make it searchable.
Do the PDF/A and accessibility checks stand in for a certificate?
No. The PDF/A check is a subset of veraPDF’s rules (20 rule groups); a clean result is not a certificate. PDF/A conversion rewrites the whole file: digital signatures stop validating and form fields are flattened into the page. A file that breaks a rule is never handed over. The PDF/UA check is automated and cannot prove conformance: reading order, the quality of alt text and changes of language need a person, and colour contrast is not measured.
Will I lose my work if I close the browser?
Unsaved changes are kept as a draft in this browser’s private file system (OPFS) only, and come back when you open the editor again; if you close the document’s tab in the editor yourself, its draft is deleted too. If the browser’s storage refuses a document’s recovery copy (when it is full, for example), the document still opens and the notice says so and why; until a draft save succeeds, unsaved changes would not survive a closed tab or a crash. Documents opened with a password and sensitive sessions leave no draft. Deleting a draft is not secure erasure; it does not overwrite the bytes on disk. For the full list of what is kept, see the Privacy page.
