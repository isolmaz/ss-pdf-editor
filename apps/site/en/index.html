All the core Acrobat-class capabilities

Reading, editing, conversion, OCR, form filling, signing and security tools come together in a single local interface.

Advanced Reading & Review

  • · Continuous scrolling, single-page, book and full-screen presentation modes
  • · Full-text search with match highlighting; thumbnails, bookmarks (outline) and document tabs
  • · A magnifier and a snapshot tool that joins the visible pages into one PNG
  • · Reading mode and read-aloud: only voices installed on your device that match the document’s language (or the interface’s, if the document declares none) are used; a cloud voice never is

Comprehensive Page Management

  • · Blank documents (A3, A4, A5, Letter, Legal); insert, delete, duplicate and replace pages
  • · Drag-and-drop reordering and rotation in 90° steps
  • · Extract pages, split, and merge several files in any order
  • · Page boxes and auto-crop, page labels

Annotation, Notes & Measurement

  • · Highlight, underline, strike-out and squiggly markup
  • · Sticky notes, freehand ink, shapes, stamps, links, images and typed text; move, rotate, resize or delete any mark
  • · Noto Sans is embedded in typed text, so Turkish letters look right in every reader
  • · Distance, perimeter and area measurement; results are written as real PDF annotations

Comments & Review

  • · Comment threads with replies and a review status (Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled, Completed)
  • · Replies and statuses are real PDF records, so Acrobat, Foxit and other readers show the same thread
  • · Import and export as XFDF, FDF and JSON
  • · Imported comments arrive as marks you can still edit

Interactive Form Filling

  • · AcroForm text fields, radio buttons, checkboxes and drop-down lists
  • · Create fields, set flags and add simple calculations
  • · Detect fields on a flat form: text fields, checkboxes, radio groups and signature fields are proposed, you confirm
  • · Permanently flatten form fields; import and export form data as FDF or JSON

XFA Forms

  • · Static XFA: filled like any form while the XFA data is updated too; a signature the form already carries keeps covering what it signed
  • · Dynamic XFA: fill it in its own window with pdf.js’s XFA renderer, save what you typed and export it as XML
  • · Import and export XFA data as the XML file Acrobat’s “Export data” writes
  • · Flatten to a normal PDF, or remove the XFA and keep only the AcroForm; XFA scripts and calculations do not run

Text Editing, Find & Replace

  • · Retype a paragraph in place and the text reflows. Only horizontal text is editable; blocks that cannot be reproduced are marked
  • · Find and replace across the document (Ctrl+H) with match case, whole words and a page range
  • · The old glyphs are really removed and the result is checked with a second PDF engine; the new text is drawn at the old size and colour
  • · Turkish and English letter case is respected (a search for “istanbul” finds its capitalised Turkish form); export text as plain text or Markdown

Convert & Create

  • · Convert Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), PowerPoint (PPTX), HTML, text/Markdown, CSV/TSV, EPUB and FB2 files to PDF in the browser
  • · Convert a PDF to an editable Word, Excel or CSV file; Word comes in three layouts: the exact layout (the default) keeps the page as it looks, with editable text boxes at the PDF’s own letter positions, the PDF’s fonts embedded where their licence allows, and shapes, pictures, colours and links in place (justified paragraphs are written left-aligned so every word stays put); flowing text you can edit at length; or one exact picture per page (pages over 22 inches are scaled down in proportion). In the exact layout a scanned page is read with OCR in the languages you choose (Turkish and English by default) and becomes editable text over its pictures, with the words OCR was unsure of marked by a Word comment; a Word or CSV file is read back and checked before it is offered, an Excel file is not; the report says what was not carried over (for Word, including how many pictures were left out)
  • · Export pages as PNG, JPEG or WebP images; build a PDF from images
  • · Conversion keeps the content, not the exact look; the old DOC, XLS and PPT formats, OpenDocument and RTF are not converted

Scan with the Camera

  • · Build a PDF from photographs taken with a phone or laptop camera, or from photo files; the picture never leaves your device
  • · The four corners of the page are found automatically; if they are not, you drag them yourself
  • · The page is straightened from its perspective; four looks: original colour, grayscale, black and white, and enhanced
  • · Multi-page scans: reorder, rotate and retake; run OCR on the result to make it searchable

OCR in 27 Languages

  • · Adds an invisible, selectable and searchable text layer to scanned pages; pages that already have text are skipped by default
  • · 27 languages: Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Swedish, Azerbaijani, Kurdish (Kurmanji), Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Greek, Arabic, Persian, Hebrew, Hindi, Chinese (simplified and traditional), Japanese and Korean
  • · Arabic, Hebrew and Chinese-Japanese-Korean text can be searched and copied too; right-to-left words come back in reading order
  • · The OCR engine and language packs are served by this site; they are downloaded the first time you use them and work offline after that

Permanent Vector Redaction & Sanitizing

  • · NOT a superficial black-box overlay
  • · Removes text operators and glyphs from the PDF stream; the output is re-checked glyph by glyph
  • · An object-level audit report: remaining terms, earlier revisions and leftover structure
  • · Sanitize: scripts, attachments, metadata, private application data, thumbnails and hidden layers; the result is re-read and verified

Digital Signing & Verification

  • · Local digital signing to the PAdES B-B standard from your PKCS#12 identity, with an optional visible stamp
  • · Drawn, typed or photographed signatures and initials; these are a picture on the page, not a certified signature
  • · Signature verification reports integrity, trust, revocation and coverage separately; RSA and ECDSA keys; trust rests only on certificates you imported
  • · Revocation is read from CRLs on your device and from revocation data embedded in the PDF, never fetched from the network; RFC 3161 timestamps are verified offline

Encryption & Passwords

  • · AES-256 password encryption with permission settings; the output is re-opened and verified
  • · A password-protected file asks for its password and opens read-only; the password is kept in memory only
  • · Remove the protection of a document whose password you know; “Create unlocked copy” opens an editable copy in a new tab
  • · Password-protected documents leave no draft, source copy or file reference on the device (sensitive session)

Accessibility & PDF/UA

  • · The quick check reports facts only: no score and no conformance claim
  • · PDF/UA check: 34 rules modelled on the Matterhorn Protocol, each marked pass, fail, check by hand, not applicable or not checked. Colour contrast is not measured
  • · A tags and reading-order editor; a tagged-PDF writer adds structure tags to an untagged file and verifies the result
  • · Alt text for images and tooltips for form fields; quick fixes for the title, language and tab order

PDF/A & Compression

  • · Convert to PDF/A-2b (the default), PDF/A-3b or PDF/A-1b; the result opens in a new tab and the original stays open
  • · A PDF/A check covering 20 rule groups also works on files you did not convert; it is not a full veraPDF validation and not a certificate
  • · A file that breaks a rule is never handed over: the operation stops and says which rule failed
  • · Compression: a structural re-save (lossless) or turning pages into images (lossy; the text layer, links and annotations are lost); if the file grows, the report says so

Document Structure & Stamping

  • · Document properties and XMP, outline (bookmark) editing
  • · Attachments, layers (OCG) and links (limited by a URI allow-list)
  • · Headers and footers, page numbers, Bates numbering
  • · Text or image watermarks and stamps

Compare, Batch & Print

  • · Compare two documents by text or by pixels; the report always says which method it used
  • · Batch processing: run one ordered set of steps over up to 256 files at a time, with a report for each file
  • · Printing: page ranges, N-up, booklet, poster and duplex sheet layouts

Workflow

  • · A home screen that lists every tool by task; the Ctrl+K palette reaches any tool
  • · Undo and redo history; local drafts for unsaved work
  • · Save over the original file (Chromium-based browsers) or export a copy
  • · Simple and advanced interface modes, a dark theme, a Turkish and English interface, and offline use

PRIVACY AND SECURITY GUARANTEE

Your documents live only on your device

SsPdfEditor is a 100% local browser tool built around user privacy.

0 Bytes Network Transfer: Your files are never transferred, copied or stored on any remote server.
No Account or Sign-up: You do not need to provide an email address, create an account or sign in.
Save Straight to Disk: In Chromium-based browsers you can write your documents directly back to the original file on your computer; in every browser you can download a copy.
Drafts Stay in This Browser: Unsaved work is kept as a draft in this browser so it is not lost; encrypted documents and sensitive sessions leave no draft. The Privacy page lists what is kept.
No Third-party Requests: The engines and fonts are served from this site, and a strict content security policy (CSP) allows connections to this site only. There are no cookies and no analytics.
Open Source: The source code is open on GitHub under AGPL-3.0-or-later, so you can check what it does.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about SsPdfEditor.

Are my files really not uploaded to any server?
Yes. You can check by opening your browser's Network tab. When you open or save a PDF, not a single byte of data is sent out. Everything happens entirely on your own computer's hardware.
How can I work without an internet connection?
The first time you open the editor, its start page, interface text and fonts are saved to your browser cache. Settings → Offline use → Prepare downloads the rest ahead: every tool's code, the PDF engines and the fonts. Once it reports ready, you can open, edit and export a PDF in airplane mode, even on a device that has never opened a PDF online. OCR language packs and the PDF/A converter work offline after one use online. Without Prepare, a tool you have not used on this device needs the internet the first time.
Why is this service 100% free?
Because all document processing runs on the user's own device, there is no expensive server-side cloud compute cost. That is why no subscription fee or restriction is needed.
How do I save the file I edited?
In desktop browsers such as Chrome or Edge you can save directly over your original file (Save). In Firefox or Safari you can download the edited result as a new file with one click (Export).
Which file and page sizes are supported?
All standard PDF documents are supported. On a computer, documents of up to 2,000 pages and 300 MB open; above 1,500 pages the editor warns you. Phones and tablets have the same ceiling, but documents over 300 pages or 64 MB open for viewing only. A batch run takes at most 256 files at a time.
Which languages does it support?
The interface is in Turkish and English; change it in Settings → Language. OCR works in 27 languages (the list is in the Features section). The OCR engine and every language pack are served by this site and downloaded the first time you use them; after that they work offline too.
Can I open Word, Excel or PowerPoint files?
Yes. DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, HTML, text/Markdown, CSV/TSV, EPUB and FB2 files are converted to PDF in the browser when you open them or drop them on the editor, and the result opens in a new tab as real, selectable and searchable text. Conversion keeps the content of the document, not its exact page layout, fonts or formatting; each report says what was approximated. The old DOC, XLS and PPT formats, OpenDocument and RTF are not converted. You can also export a PDF to Word, Excel or CSV; a PDF records no paragraphs or tables, so these are rebuilt from the page and can be wrong. In the exact Word layout a scanned page is read with OCR (Turkish and English unless you choose others), so its text becomes editable; words OCR was unsure of get a Word comment.
Is redaction really permanent?
Yes. Marked text is not covered with a black box; it is removed from the PDF content, the file is rewritten and the output is re-checked glyph by glyph. The audit report lists any remaining terms, earlier revisions and leftover structure. Sanitize removes what the pages do not show (scripts, attachments, metadata, hidden layers) and verifies the result by reading it back; a digital signature does not survive it. A limit: the audit scans raw bytes, so it cannot see inside compressed streams, and the report says so.
What is the difference between a simple signature and a digital signature?
A drawn, typed or photographed signature is a picture placed on the page; it proves nothing about who signed or whether the document changed afterwards, and the dialog says so. For that, sign with your PKCS#12 identity to get a PAdES B-B signature. A new signature carries no timestamp and no revocation data, because there is no network to ask. When verifying, trust rests only on certificates you imported and revocation is read only from lists on your device and from revocation data embedded in the PDF itself; with neither, the answer is “unknown”.
Can I scan a document with my phone?
Yes: File → Scan with camera (also on the home screen and in the Ctrl+K palette). On a phone it opens the rear camera, the picture never leaves your device and the camera runs only while you scan. With no camera, or if you refuse permission, you can choose photo files instead. The camera needs a secure connection (https or localhost). The page is found from its edges and straightened; the output is not meant for archival reproduction. You can run OCR on the resulting PDF to make it searchable.
Do the PDF/A and accessibility checks stand in for a certificate?
No. The PDF/A check is a subset of veraPDF’s rules (20 rule groups); a clean result is not a certificate. PDF/A conversion rewrites the whole file: digital signatures stop validating and form fields are flattened into the page. A file that breaks a rule is never handed over. The PDF/UA check is automated and cannot prove conformance: reading order, the quality of alt text and changes of language need a person, and colour contrast is not measured.
Will I lose my work if I close the browser?
Unsaved changes are kept as a draft in this browser’s private file system (OPFS) only, and come back when you open the editor again; if you close the document’s tab in the editor yourself, its draft is deleted too. If the browser’s storage refuses a document’s recovery copy (when it is full, for example), the document still opens and the notice says so and why; until a draft save succeeds, unsaved changes would not survive a closed tab or a crash. Password-protected documents and sensitive sessions leave no draft. Deleting a draft is not secure erasure; it does not overwrite the bytes on disk. For the full list of what is kept, see the Privacy page.
